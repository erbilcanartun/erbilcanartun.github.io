Signals and networks

Reading Earth’s magnetic fluctuations

Earth’s magnetic field fluctuates even on a quiet day. When an instrument picks up an unusual fluctuation, can measurements from other stations help us work out where it came from?

In the DETAM project at TÜBİTAK UME, I develop methods for comparing time series from search-coil and fluxgate magnetometers. I use signal processing and anomaly detection to look for patterns that stand out across stations. Whether any of them can be reliably associated with seismic activity is an open question.
