Biophysics

What does natural selection cost in energy?

Individual cells spend energy to stay alive and reproduce. How does that expenditure enter the picture when we zoom out and study natural selection in a population?

I am investigating this question with Efe Ilker and Michael Hinczewski. Our ongoing collaboration connects two descriptions of the same biological process, one in terms of cellular metabolism and the other in terms of how heritable traits change in frequency across generations.
