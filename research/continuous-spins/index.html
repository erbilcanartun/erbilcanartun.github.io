Magnetism and phase transitions

In how many ways can rotating spins order in two dimensions?

Place two freely rotating spins at every site of a flat lattice. One can follow either spin across the lattice, or form a variable that combines the two and follow that instead. Those choices reveal different patterns of order.

We calculated how the interactions in this XY–Ashkin–Teller model change under repeated rescaling. Four phases retained correlations that fade as a power of distance. They differed in whether the individual or combined spins favored alignment or alternation. Each phase was associated with a continuous family of scale-invariant interactions, allowing the rate of correlation decay to vary within a phase.

Publication

E. C. Artun, A. N. Berker Multiplicity of Algebraic Order from Fixed Lines of Potential Surfaces: XY-Ashkin-Teller in Spatial Dimension d=2 Physical Review E, 2025
