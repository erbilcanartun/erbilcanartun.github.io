Is there chaos in a relaxing current?

Even at constant voltage, the current through a glassy semiconductor can take time to settle. Does the irregular part of that relaxation retain information about the state that produced it?

We reconstructed possible trajectories from delayed copies of the measured current in two chalcogenide films. Nearby trajectories separated at a positive estimated rate in both samples, with largest Lyapunov exponents of 0.317 and 0.456. This was evidence of sensitivity to initial conditions in these traces. The journal article also examines how correlations change between shorter and longer observation intervals.
