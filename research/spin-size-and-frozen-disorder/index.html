Magnetism and phase transitions

Does the size of a spin change glassy order?

In a spin glass, some neighbors favor alignment and others favor opposition. They can freeze in an irregular pattern because no arrangement satisfies all the bonds. Does the familiar two-state model of this effect still work when each spin has many possible values?

We followed the effective bond strengths as we grouped spins into successively larger blocks. On a three-dimensional hierarchical lattice, the glassy phase survived all the way from spin one-half to the infinite-spin limit. Even the rate at which nearby calculations diverged under rescaling stayed the same. Its Lyapunov exponent was about 1.93 throughout the series.

Publication

E. C. Artun, A. N. Berker Spin-s Spin-Glass Phases in the d=3 Ising Model Physical Review E, 2021arXiv
