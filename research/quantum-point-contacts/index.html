Electronic transport

How does a magnetic field steer electrons through a narrow constriction?

Squeeze a sheet of electrons through a sufficiently narrow opening and only a few conducting channels fit. In my undergraduate thesis, I asked how a magnetic field changes the way those channels carry current through a quantum point contact.

I solved the three-dimensional electrostatic problem self-consistently, allowing the charge distribution and electric potential to adjust to each other. In the simulated device, geometry affected where the current flowed. Varying the magnetic field alone could move the system between nonequilibrium and equilibrated transport.
