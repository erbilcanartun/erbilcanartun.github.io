Do countries with similar governance have similar pandemic waves?

Two countries can experience similar rhythms of COVID-19 cases and deaths even when their institutions differ. In an unpublished collaboration, we asked how often such similarities appeared and whether they followed patterns in governance.

We compared case and death records from 180 countries between January 2020 and February 2022. Using the Spin Glass Machine, we matched the fluctuations at different time scales and plotted countries with similar patterns near one another. Countries in the same governance quartile formed several smaller clusters, and some countries with different profiles occupied the same point. These overlaps limit how confidently any one indicator can explain a country’s trajectory.
